# study_design_section_criteria.md

Section purpose:
Provide a brief, accurate study design orientation while avoiding unnecessary protocol detail and appropriately cross-referring to the protocol/CSP as needed.

Section-specific success criteria:
- captures only essential design-defining elements
- remains lean and non-redundant
- avoids unsupported methodological descriptors
- aligns with approved abbreviated CSR style where relevant
- uses appropriate appendix/CSP cross-reference when expected

Additional dimensions to consider:
- essential_design_element_coverage
- brevity_and_non_redundancy
- cross_reference_appropriateness
- precedent_style_alignment

Section-specific critical failure triggers:
- incorrect phase or study type
- incorrect randomization, blinding, or control description
- major omission of a design-defining feature
- unsupported methodological descriptor
- misleadingly detailed or misleadingly sparse summary
- incorrect appendix/CSP cross-reference where required

Weighting adjustment guidance:
Increase weight on:
- source faithfulness
- section purpose fit
- detail level fit

Practical note:
For lean Study Design sections, over-generation may be as problematic as under-generation.
