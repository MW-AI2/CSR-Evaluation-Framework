# synopsis_section_criteria.md

## Section Purpose
Provide a concise, accurate, high-level summary of the study and its key findings in a form appropriate for the CSR synopsis.

## Section-Specific Success Criteria
- captures the most important study information accurately
- remains appropriately concise
- reflects the intended high-level summary function
- avoids introducing detail that belongs in later sections
- maintains consistency across design, efficacy, and safety messaging

## Additional Dimensions to Consider
- summary selectivity
- cross-domain consistency
- concision and balance
- high-level message discipline

## Section-Specific Critical Failure Triggers
- omission of a major study-defining fact or major result
- inclusion of unsupported headline claim
- materially imbalanced representation of efficacy or safety
- contradiction between major synopsis statements and source

## Weighting Adjustment Guidance
Increase emphasis on:
- source faithfulness
- section purpose fit
- completeness

## Practical Note
Synopsis evaluation should emphasize balance and disciplined high-level framing.
